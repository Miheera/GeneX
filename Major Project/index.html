About

Benchmarks how well each ranking method (betweenness, degree, impact score) recovers known COSMIC breast cancer driver genes in the network's top-N ranked genes.

Higher precision means fewer false positives; higher recall means more drivers are captured. F1 is the harmonic mean.

Interpretation

Spearman rho tests whether genes with higher expression tend to have higher structural impact. It describes association only; it does not establish causation.
